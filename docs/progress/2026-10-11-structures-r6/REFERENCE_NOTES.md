# Reference notes (round 6). Opened with Read: street/13, 11, 12, 04, 05, 14 and aerial/26 (7 photos, docs/reference/, not copied here)

- street/13 embankment: a flat mown verge about half a lane wide, then one long straight bank (roughly 1:2.5); the toe meets the verge in a soft curve a couple of metres long; the crest against the fence is a broad round-over, about a quarter of the bank length. No ditch.
- street/14 embankment: same pattern at about 1:3; toe and crest flow with no visible crease; verge about half a lane.
- street/11 chalk cutting (overhead): verge strip, then a straight steep face, with a soft crest into the field. The toe is a narrow strip, not a ditch.
- street/12 vegetated cutting: verge about half a lane, then a steep bank; crest hidden by planting.
- street/04 netted rock face: the wall stops against the rock face with a vertical end; the face goes on above it. There is no ramp cap.
- street/05 gabion wall: flat top with a narrow flat strip behind it, then the bank. The wall ends against the bank with a stepped vertical end and the bank wraps round it. Wall ends are blunt and buried, not ramped.
- aerial/26: construction-stage earthworks: banks with rounded crests, no hard creases; nothing on wall ends or side-by-side roads.

Measurements are by eye and scale-free. They agree with a rounding of about a quarter to a third of the slope length at crest and toe (the bound used here: one third) and a verge of about half a lane (1.5 m is right). They do NOT support a ramp cap on wall ends: photos 04 and 05 show vertical ends buried into the bank, so a ramp cap was not built.
No photo covers: a benched rock cut, two roads at different levels side by side, a retaining-wall end seen from above. No new photos were downloaded (out of time).
