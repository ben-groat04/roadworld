# Vegetation kit (procedural Blender meshes), 11 Oct 2026

Blender 5.2.2 LTS (Blender 4.2 also installed; 5.2 ships no tree generator in `addons_core` and the Sapling add-on is reported broken on 5.x, so trees use our own generator). No downloads, no add-ons. Everything is regenerated by script.

## How it is built
* **Hedges**: opaque lumpy core (superellipse section, periodic noise, `veg_core`) plus leaf-cluster cards scattered over the core faces with randomised orientation, outward-biased vertex normals and a shade hint in vertex colour B; overgrown adds bulges, height variation and emergent shoots (thin tubes with cards). Chosen over a displaced shell because the silhouette comes from cards poking out, nothing is see-through, and tris stay 2-7k; from 5-50 m the dark core plus patchy shade reads as depth.
* **Trees**: recursive branching skeleton (levels 0-3, 4 for L: per-level counts, angles, length ratios, gnarl, up tropism, height envelope) built once per (species, size, seed); LOD0 renders all levels with leaf-cluster cards at branch ends (outward shading normals, depth-based AO), LOD1 drops the last level and halves clumps with bigger cards, LOD2 is trunk plus clustered opaque lumps. Conifer: leader plus whorls of drooping spray cards. Sapling: thin leader, few twigs, square stake.
* **Textures**: numpy-rasterised leaf/blade shapes with supersampling, base colour (alpha mask), height-derived normal, roughness. Atlas cells: leaf atlas 2x2 (0 broadleaf cluster, 1 hedge leaves, 2 conifer spray, 3 gorse spray); grass atlas 4x2 (0 mown, 1 rough, 2 tussock, 3 weed rosette, 4 bracken frond, 5 dry blades, 6 creep ground patch).
* **Conventions**: metres, FBX 1 m = 100 uu, +X along run, +Y toward carriageway, Z up, pivot at ground contact. Slots: `veg_leaf` and `veg_grass` (two-sided masked), `veg_bark`, `veg_core`, `veg_canopy_far`, `veg_timber` (opaque). Colour attribute `Wind`: R wind weight (0 base to 1 tips), G per-card phase (the preview also uses it as a hue shift), B shade/AO hint (preview multiplies it into the colour). Custom normals are exported.

## Known weaknesses (honest)
* Hedge cards still read as plates close up (scene a closeup): leaf cluster texture is cartoon-like, no real leaf-size detail, and the core shows as dark flat patches where cards thin out. Trimmed hedges are still close to boxes; real trimmed hedges have more stray shoots and a stepped top. No blossom, no bare winter state.
* Trees are placeholders: round tree reads well, open tree is thin and a little "ash-like lollipop" at M and L; conifer is stylised tiers and dark; sapling crown is tiny. LOD2 is faceted lumps (visible as boulders against the sky at 100 m+). No bare or autumn leaf states built.
* Grass cards show their flat planes at grazing angles; mown strips need about 40 clumps per m2 (10k instances in scene a) which is only workable inside about 30 m; far verge should be a ground material. The creep strip looks like a tile, not like natural encroachment.
* Preview materials are Cycles approximations: Unreal needs its own foliage materials (two-sided, masked, subsurface, per-instance tint, wind from `Wind.R`). Preview ground/asphalt are flat colours and reflect a blue sky, so overall colour looks cool; judge shape and density, not colour.
* Hedge/shrub tint variety relies on vertex colour G and per-instance tint. LOD transitions were chosen by distance only in these scenes; no cross-fade.
* Nanite is not needed for these (foliage as masked cards); do not enable Nanite on the card meshes without testing.

## What the Unreal import step needs
One FBX per LOD: import each `SM_Veg_<Type>_<Var>_LOD0` as the static mesh and attach LOD1/LOD2 files as its LODs (`EditorStaticMeshLibrary.set_lods` or import with LOD index); `unreal/tools/py/import_kit_meshes.py` handles only the Kit manifest, so a sibling script reading `vegetation_manifest.json` is needed (combine_meshes true, build_nanite off, normals imported from file, vertex colours imported, no collision, scale 1). Materials: map slots `veg_leaf`/`veg_grass` to masked two-sided foliage materials using `T_Veg_Leaf_*`/`T_Veg_Grass_*` (BC alpha as mask, sRGB on BC only, normal and roughness as linear), `veg_core`/`veg_bark`/`veg_timber`/`veg_canopy_far` to opaque materials; read vertex colour R for wind, B as AO, add per-instance tint via custom data. Check axis mirroring on first import (same note as the Kit script). Hedge modules should be instanced with +X along the polyline tangent, ends E00/E01 mirrored by yaw 180.
