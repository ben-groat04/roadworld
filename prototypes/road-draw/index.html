Road Draw prototype

Double-click / Enter / right-clickFinish road
Esc / BackspaceCancel road / remove last point
Hold ShiftSnapping off (angle, length, guidelines, roads)
PgUp / PgDn or ] / [Raise / lower the next point 1 m (Shift: 5 m). In edit mode: the hovered handle.
